## Overview
Study Current is a private desktop rhythm for focused work: choose an intention, work in a short cycle, then leave a single reflection for your future self.

## Build Story
### A quieter timer | Nov 14 | idea
I wanted something that asks less of you than a productivity dashboard.

### Reflections, not scores | Nov 15 | feature
I added a one-line end-of-session note instead of a productivity score.

### First desktop build | Nov 16 | deployment
Packaging the project made it feel real—and revealed several platform-specific issues.

### A tool I still use | Nov 16 | final
The final version became part of my own study routine.
