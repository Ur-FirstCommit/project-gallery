## Overview
Circuit Quest transforms basic electrical ideas into playful rooms. Players connect components, see what happens, and learn through small, satisfying discoveries.

## Build Story
### Learn by trying | Nov 14 | idea
We wanted circuit concepts to be something you play with, not just memorise.

### First light | Nov 14 | commit
Our first level only had a battery, wire and bulb — but it proved the interaction felt good.

### Puzzle rules emerge | Nov 15 | feature
We built small challenges that introduce one new idea at a time.

### Five rooms to explore | Nov 16 | final
The submission included five handcrafted puzzle rooms and a simple soundscape.

## What I Learned
I learned to build the smallest playable version first. Once one light bulb worked, every new mechanic had a place to grow from.

## Challenges
### Explaining a failed puzzle attempt without spoiling it.
How I solved it: We used short observations like “that path has no power” and let players make the next connection.
