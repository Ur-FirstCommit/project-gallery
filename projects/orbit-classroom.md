## Overview
Orbit Classroom helps teachers turn the wonder of space into small, usable lessons. Students can explore planetary data, compare distances, and save a mission card for their next class.

## Build Story
### A lesson with a sense of scale | Jul 18 | idea
We wanted the solar system to feel less like a chart and more like a place students could explore.

### First commit | Jul 18 | commit
A simple orbital map and a tiny API experiment became the first working prototype.

### Mission cards take shape | Jul 19 | feature
We added printable prompts that connect a planet fact to a short classroom activity.

### Ready for launch | Jul 20 | final
After testing with a teacher, we simplified the language and shipped our first version.

## What I Learned
I learned how to make an API request useful instead of just displaying the response. We also learned to make decisions quickly by testing an early version with someone outside our team.

## Challenges
### Making big space distances understandable.
How I solved it: We used comparisons and a deliberately simple visual scale rather than trying to be scientifically literal everywhere.

## AI Usage
Categories: Learning, Brainstorming
