## Overview
ShelterLink puts essential details first: opening times, accessibility notes, availability and directions. It is a prototype for an experience that remains legible under pressure.

## Build Story
### Design for urgency | May 9 | idea
We listed the information a person would need in under ten seconds.

### The map becomes a list | May 10 | feature
We made the list view the default after learning that maps hide important accessibility details.

### A focused prototype | May 11 | final
We presented a compact flow centred on clarity, accessibility and fast decisions.

## What I Learned
I learned that accessibility is not a final checklist. It shaped what we chose to show, the words we used, and how the screens were ordered.

## Challenges
### Choosing what to leave out.
How I solved it: I made every detail earn its place by asking whether it would help someone decide where to go.
